Financial Literacy

What you'll learn
  • Saving
  • Budgeting
  • Investing
  • Assets vs. liabilities
  • Compound interest
The big idea

Money you understand is money that works for you. Learning how saving, investing, and compound interest work early gives you options later — the goal isn't just earning, it's building something that grows on its own.

🧠

Critical Thinking

What you'll learn
  • Decision making
  • Evaluating evidence
  • Logical thinking
  • Solving problems
  • Avoiding misinformation
Why it matters

In a world full of information — and misinformation — knowing how to question, evaluate, and reason clearly is one of the most future-proof skills there is.
